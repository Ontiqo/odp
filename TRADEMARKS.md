# Omninput's name and logo

The code here is open under the Mozilla Public License 2.0: take it, change it, and build screens and senders on it.
The name **Omninput** and the Omninput logo belong to Ontiqo Pty Ltd and are not part of that licence.

## What you may say

- That your app, device or kiosk is **for Omninput** or **works with Omninput**, so people know it fits with
  Omninput computers. For example: "Lounge Screen, works with Omninput" or "KioskOS for Omninput".
- That it is based on Omninput Screen: "based on the Open Display Protocol (MPL 2.0)".

## What you may not do

- Name your app, device, company or project **Omninput**, or anything that reads as Omninput's own (such as
  "Omninput Screen Pro" or "Omninput TV").
- Use the Omninput logo or icon, or anything closely like it, as your app's icon or in your branding.
- Say or suggest that Ontiqo made, endorses, supports or is involved in your work, unless Ontiqo has agreed in
  writing.

## Working with Ontiqo

Ontiqo may list, link to or support work built on these apps, and may agree to an official name for it. Ask at
ontiqo.com.
